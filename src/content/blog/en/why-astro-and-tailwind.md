Building a personal website does not always require a complex backend architecture or a heavy database.

## Why Choose a Static Site?

- **Fast by default:** There is no database query or server-side processing required for every page view.
- **Smaller attack surface:** A static website has fewer moving parts to secure and maintain.
- **Simple deployment:** The generated site can be deployed to platforms such as Vercel or Cloudflare Pages.
